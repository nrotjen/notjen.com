ABOUT

I'm Nicholas Ottjen, currently in the BRED program at USC.

I have the somewhat arrogant ambition of becoming one of the great real estate developers of my generation.

I want to build mixed-use destinations that become attractions in their own right — places that people deliberately choose to visit, rather than projects that simply ride someone else's coattails or depend on whatever economic trend happens to be favorable at the time.

I believe great development requires more than assembling land, capital, and tenants. It requires a point of view. The best projects create something that did not previously exist and, when successful, become part of the identity of the place in which they are built.

That is what I want to spend my career doing.

I am particularly interested in commercial real estate, architecture, urbanism, retail, hospitality, and the relationship between the physical environment and the businesses and people that occupy it.

I am currently studying real estate at the University of Southern California through the Bachelor of Real Estate Development program.

Eventually, I intend to develop projects of consequence.

If you are interested in investing, partnering, developing, or simply having a conversation about an interesting project, please contact me.
